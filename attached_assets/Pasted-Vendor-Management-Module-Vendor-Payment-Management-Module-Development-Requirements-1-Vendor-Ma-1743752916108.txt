2. Vendor Payment Management Module
Client Payment Verification Dashboard:
Real-time payment status tracker (Received, Pending, Delayed)
Automated Notifications for pending client payments
Client Payment Confirmation: Accounts team marks payments as "Received" to trigger vendor payments
Historical Data Access for past client payments
Vendor Attendance and Payment Calculation:
Monthly attendance tracking (submitted by vendors/POCs by the 25th)
Automated Payment Calculation based on attended days and approved budget
Leave Approval Workflow: POCs approve/reject leave requests
Payment Summary Report (Downloadable report with attended days, deductions, final amount)
Vendor Invoice and Email Integration:
Invoice Upload by vendors/POCs (with automated reminders)
Email Integration: Automatically attach vendor correspondence to profiles
Invoice Verification Tool: Flags discrepancies between invoices and calculated payments
Audit Trail: Log of all invoice uploads, emails, and verification actions
Payment Approval Workflow:
Conditional Approval Process: Payments approved only after client payment confirmation, attendance verification, and invoice validation
Approval Dashboard for Dilipbhai: Displays client payment status, calculated payment, invoices, and discrepancies for one-click approval
Automated Payment Notification to accounts team upon approval
Internal POC Accountability and Data Submission:
Data Submission Deadline: POCs submit attendance, invoices, and emails by the 25th (reminders on 20th, 23rd, and 25th)
POC Dashboard: Displays pending tasks, submitted data, and payment status for vendors
Escalation Mechanism if deadlines are missed
Vendor Payment History and Reporting:
Searchable Payment History Log
Monthly Payment Report summarizing vendor payments, deductions, and client payment status
Discrepancy Tracking with resolution details
